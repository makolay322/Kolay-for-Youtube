Features

Small fixes that add up.

Turn on only what you need. Every feature has its own switch, and the popup keeps the most used ones one click away.

Speed hotkeys — on every site

Not only YouTube: any video on any website. Your last speed is remembered browser-wide, pitch is preserved, and a small badge shows the new speed. Keys and step size are yours to change.

Summarize with Gemini

A ✦ button on every video tile and on the watch page. One click reads the transcript and opens Gemini with a ready prompt. Long videos work too: the transcript is attached as a file.

Your prompt, your language

Short summary, study notes, Q&A or “is it worth watching?”, or write your own. Pick the summary language and add timestamps that link into the video.

Quality that sticks

Pick 4K, 1080p or anything else once. If it isn't available, the next best one is chosen — and the high-bitrate version when there is one.

Hide Shorts & clutter

Shorts everywhere, Mixes, surveys, Playables, “Explore new topics”, live streams on Subscriptions, watched videos — each one separately.

Repeat hider

Recommendations you've already scrolled past three times quietly disappear, so your home page finally shows something new.

Playlist cleaner

Empty Watch Later or any playlist you own with one click, instead of removing hundreds of videos by hand.

Your own filters

Block channels and title keywords (regex too), hide videos by length, members-only and premieres. Dim instead of hide if you prefer.

Player that behaves

Default volume, captions on or off, no autoplay, theater mode, pause when the tab is hidden or another video starts.

Cleaner layout

Hide comments, sidebar, end cards, live chat or promo strips. Set videos per row and your own progress bar color.

Settings

Every detail, one searchable page.

Fine-tune thresholds, shortcuts and filters. Back up all settings to a JSON file and restore them on another computer.

  • Instant search across all options
  • Export / import as JSON
  • Light and dark theme, follows your system
  • Exclude pages like History or your own playlists
  • In 8 languages: English, Türkçe, Español, Português, Deutsch, Français, Русский, 日本語

Your data stays yours.

Kolay for YouTube has no server, no analytics and no account. It never sends anything to the developer — settings live in your browser's own storage. Only the optional Gemini summary talks to YouTube and to the Gemini tab you open. Read the privacy policy.

  • No data collection of any kind
  • No servers, no remote code
  • One permission: storage

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Why does it ask to “read and change data on all websites”?

Only because the speed hotkeys work on videos on every site. That part of the extension does nothing but listen for your shortcut keys and change the playback speed of <video> elements. It reads nothing else and sends nothing anywhere. If you don't need that, you can limit site access from your browser's extension settings.

Is it free? Are there ads?

Free, with no ads, no premium tier and no tracking. The full source code is on GitHub.

Does it work with YouTube Premium?

Yes. With Premium, the quality setting can even pick the “enhanced bitrate” version automatically.

Something stopped working after a YouTube update.

YouTube changes its layout often. Please open an issue on GitHub; turning on Settings → General → Debug log helps a lot.

Does the Gemini summary send my data somewhere?

Only where you send it. The extension reads the video's transcript from YouTube and types it, with a prompt, into a Gemini tab that you open. Nothing goes through a server of ours. The copy button keeps it all on your clipboard instead, and you can turn the feature off completely.

Will my settings survive an update?

Yes. Settings are kept across updates, and you can export them to a file at any time.

Give YouTube a quiet upgrade.

Free for Chrome and Firefox. Takes a few seconds.
